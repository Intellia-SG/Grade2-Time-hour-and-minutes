1. Executive Summary
Time: Hours & Minutes is a gamified, story-driven educational web platform designed to teach Grade 2 students (ages 7–8) the foundational concepts of telling time — reading hours and minutes on both analog and digital clocks. The platform transforms a traditionally abstract skill into an immersive interactive adventure, aligning with the best practices of Singapore Math, Common Core (USA), UK Key Stage 1, Australian Curriculum, and CBSE/ICSE standards.
1.1 Problem Statement
Telling time is one of the most challenging early mathematics skills. It requires simultaneous comprehension of circular number arrangement, the dual-speed motion of clock hands, the relationship between hours and minutes, and conversion between analog and digital formats. Traditional worksheet-based instruction leads to rote memorization without conceptual understanding.
Children often:
Confuse the hour and minute hands
Struggle to connect analog clock readings to digital displays
Lack intuition for how many minutes have passed
Feel anxious and disengaged with traditional drill practice

1.2 Vision
🌟 PLATFORM VISION
Time: Hours & Minutes transforms telling time from a rote memorization exercise into a joyful discovery adventure. Every child should feel like a time-traveling explorer who genuinely understands the rhythm of hours and minutes — not just someone who can recite answers.

6.2 Detailed Module: Module 5 — Minute World (Sample Deep Dive)
This module is the most cognitively demanding and follows a precise instructional sequence:
Pre-Knowledge Check (Screen 5.0)
Recall gate: student must demonstrate they can identify o'clock and half-past before entering
Gentle reminder animation if needed — not a barrier

Discovery Phase (Screens 5.1–5.2)
Pip spins the minute hand slowly around the clock
Student taps each number as Pip points — reveals: "5, 10, 15… each number is 5 minutes!"
Interactive: student can drag Pip's finger around the clock to hear the skip-count themselves

Concept Delivery (Screens 5.3–5.4)
Animated visualization: minute hand moves from 12 to 1 → "5 minutes!" pop appears
Each segment from number to number animated as a glowing arc
Student taps any number to see its minute value highlighted

Guided Practice (Screens 5.5–5.6)
Clock shown; minute hand at a number; student selects the minute value from 3 options
Hint system: tapping ? shows the counting path animated again
Error response: clock gently shakes; Pip says "Let's count the jumps together!"

Independent Practice (Screens 5.7–5.8)
5 questions — full time reading (hour + minute) required
Adaptive: if 3+ errors, reduce to minute-only questions before returning to full time reading

Module Assessment (Screen 5.9)
8 questions across all minute values (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 00)
Mastery threshold: 80% (6 of 8 correct) to earn 3-star rating

12. Appendix
12.1 Glossary
Term
Definition
Analog Clock
Traditional clock with a circular face, numbered 1–12, with hour and minute hands
Digital Clock
Clock displaying time as numerals (e.g., 3:45)
O'clock
When the minute hand points to 12 (e.g., 3:00 = "three o'clock")
Half-past
When minute hand points to 6; 30 minutes past the hour (e.g., 3:30)
Quarter past
15 minutes past the hour; minute hand at 3 (e.g., 3:15)
Quarter to
15 minutes before the next hour; minute hand at 9 (e.g., 3:45)
A.M.
Ante meridiem — the 12 hours from midnight to noon
P.M.
Post meridiem — the 12 hours from noon to midnight
Elapsed Time
The amount of time that has passed between a start and end time
LO
Learning Objective — a specific, measurable skill to be mastered
MVU
Minimum Viable Unit — a single self-contained learning module
